# Community Guidelines

Yive is a technical project for coordinating local CLI agents. Keep discussion
focused, respectful, and useful to people trying to build or debug the tool.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but keep technical claims specific and testable.
- Share enough context for others to reproduce bugs or evaluate designs.
- Respect security and privacy boundaries. Do not post private logs, tokens,
  or workspace contents.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Repeated off-topic comments after maintainers ask to refocus.
- Posting exploit details or secrets in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation
when behavior makes the project harder to maintain safely.

For security-sensitive issues, follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
